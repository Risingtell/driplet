# Friends "pay from your own wallet" cheat-sheet

Goal: get 3–5 friends to each pay from their **own** Face ID wallet, on different phones.
That turns "thousands of drips from one demo wallet" into "real different people paid me",
which is the single most credible thing to show a judge.

---

## The message to send (copy-paste this into WhatsApp)

> Hey 👋 I built something and I need a small favour, takes about 2 minutes on your phone.
>
> It's called Driplet. You watch a live stream and the creator gets paid by the second,
> in real crypto (USDC). It's on a testnet, so it's play money, not your real money. I
> just want to show that real people can pay, not just me.
>
> Here's all you do:
>
> 1. Open this link on your phone 👉 trydriplet.vercel.app/watch/ada-live
> 2. Tap the big play button and watch the money tick up for a few seconds.
> 3. Tap *"Continue with Face ID"* and approve with your face/fingerprint. It makes you a
>    free wallet, no app, no password to remember.
> 4. It'll show a wallet address and a button: tap *"Copy address & open faucet.circle.com"*.
> 5. On that faucet page: choose *Arc Testnet*, paste the address (it's already copied),
>    and tap to get the free test USDC.
> 6. Come back to Driplet and tap *"I've funded it, continue"*.
> 7. That's it, you're now watching and paying from your own wallet 🎉
>
> Send me a screenshot when you see "Paying from your wallet" and you've made my day.
> Thank you 🙏

---

## Notes for you (don't send these)
- They need a phone with Face ID / fingerprint (any modern iPhone or Android). On a laptop
  it uses Touch ID / Windows Hello.
- The faucet gives 20 test USDC per address every 2 hours, plenty.
- If a friend gets stuck at the faucet, the one thing to check is they picked **Arc Testnet**,
  not another network.
- Aim for 3 to 5 friends on different phones. That's all you need for the distinct-wallet proof.
- Each real payment shows up on **trydriplet.vercel.app/impact** with its on-chain Gateway ID,
  so you can point judges there as verification.
